I've been meaning to start a technical blog for a while. Not to teach — I'm still very much learning — but because writing is how I solidify understanding.

## The Format

Every post here starts with a real problem I ran into:

- Something broke and I didn't immediately know why
- A concept didn't click until I dug deeper
- A project revealed a gap in my knowledge

Then I walk through the process: what I tried, what failed, what actually worked, and what I'd do differently.

## What I'm Focusing On

I'm aiming for **Java backend**, **DevOps/cloud**, and **cybersecurity** roles. Posts will live in those spaces:

- Spring Boot internals
- Docker, Linux, CI/CD
- CTF writeups and network concepts
- AWS and cloud infrastructure

## Why Publish It

Two reasons. First, explaining things forces precision — you can't bluff your way through a written walkthrough. Second, the post that would have saved me three hours of debugging might save someone else the same.
