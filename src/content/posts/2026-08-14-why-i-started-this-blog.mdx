Active notetaking during studying can be a useful tool in the whole `active recall` learning process. Notetaking during reading, learning, making projects has been a standard for me for quite some time and one day i realized - why not just keep the most important entries from my draft notes as a personal journal documenting my progress and being a base of knowledge for future.

## Why Publish It?
Most of the contents I'm going to write about are technical issues related to the IT sphere. If I spend time researching and writing down notes to get a better understanding of cyber-world, I can share my work and `maybe somebody else will also find this useful`.

## The Format

Every post here starts with a real problem I ran into:

- Something broke and I didn't immediately know why
- A concept didn't click until I dug deeper
- A project revealed a gap in my knowledge

Then I walk through the process: what I tried, what failed, what actually worked, and what I'd do differently.
